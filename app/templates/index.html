A FIVE-MINUTE TOUR

From first inquiry to finished job.

  1. Review an inquiry. Open Inbox and prepare an editable sample job brief.
  2. Build a quote. Save illustrative line items and approve the current revision internally.
  3. Plan the work. Assign a crew and equipment. Conflicting dates are blocked.
  4. Keep everyone informed. Track progress, save notes and prepare a customer update draft.
  5. Explore the connections. Preview local data mappings for accounting, calendar and CRM.

The assistant uses prepared examples and saved job facts. No live AI calls, external connections or messages are sent.
