# A five-minute client walkthrough

Open https://frank-operations-demo.onrender.com before the call. The free service may need time to wake after inactivity.

**Opening:** “This is an independent concept for your operations team. All projects and prices are fictional. The workflow and persistence are working; the assistant uses prepared examples, and integrations are local previews.”

## 1. Understand the day — Overview

Point out active jobs, the inquiry queue, draft quote value and upcoming assignments. These numbers come from records in the current workspace. Open the built-in Demo walkthrough for a guided summary.

## 2. Review an inquiry — Inbox

Select Cedar Ridge access road and choose **Prepare job brief**. Show the customer, site, service and requested dates. Mention the missing-information checklist. Edit the site if desired, then choose **Create draft job**.

Explain that the known sample inquiry provides the suggested brief. It is not a live model response.

## 3. Build an internally approved quote — Job workspace

Set quantity to 2 and unit price to 125.55. Save the revision; the subtotal is **CAD 251.10 before tax**. Approve the saved quote internally.

Optional: save a revised price before scheduling and show the retained quote history. The new revision requires approval again. Unsaved edits cannot be approved.

## 4. Assign the resources

Choose a crew, equipment and the requested dates. Schedule the job. To demonstrate conflict protection, try dates already assigned to the same crew or equipment in Schedule; the app explains which reservation conflicts.

Choose **Start job**, add an operational note, then **Mark completed**. These transitions update the timeline and lock the quote and assignment.

## 5. Prepare a customer update

Choose **Prepare customer update**. Show that the draft uses the saved site, status and assignment. Edit the draft, save it, then use **Copy message**.

Nothing is emailed. If clipboard access is unavailable, the message remains selectable for manual copying.

## 6. Preview a future integration

In Integrations, select the demonstrated job. Preview CRM, QuickBooks or email/calendar data. QuickBooks requires a saved quote; the calendar preview requires an assignment.

Point out **Demo / Not connected**, the local record timestamp, and sent: false. The payload is illustrative and no external API was called.

## Closing

Refresh the page to show that edits persist. On mobile, Schedule switches to an assignment list. Use **Reset sample data** only when ready to replace the current visitor's work with fresh examples; other visitor workspaces remain intact.

“Next, we would validate your real quoting and dispatch rules, choose one integration, and test it using your authorized sandbox account.”
